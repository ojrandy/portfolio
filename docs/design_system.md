# Design System — OJ City

## The idea in one line
> **OJ City** is a small, living city at the foot of Fako, the way Buea is. Every building is something Ojong built, and the city changes with Buea's real time and weather.

Principles: **simple but alive** · **real data only** · **calm and professional, with personality in the details** · **fast first, beautiful second**.

## Interactive city (Phase 3-B, ADR-013)
Draft rules; each task refines its section when it's built.
- Vehicles are built in code from simple shapes, flat-shaded in the palette, so they match the buildings (no imported model packs). One `InstancedMesh` per type (colour per instance): at most 11 draw calls for any number of vehicles. No brand names or logos.
- Vehicle kit (P3B-02, `src/lib/vehicles.js` → `src/city/vehicleGeometry.js` → `Vehicles.jsx`): each type is a list of boxes, cylinders (wheels, logs) and low-poly blobs (banana bunches) merged into one geometry with vertex colours; a vehicle faces +z. Body parts take the instance colour (a seeded variant: the same seed always gives the same colour); glass, tyres, trim and lamps keep theirs. Every type is at most 600 triangles and fits a street lane (widest: bus 0.72; longest: bus 2.24). Types (11): sedan (1.04 long), SUV, pickup (open bed), taxi (sedan shape, always taxi yellow `#F2C21B`, plain like Cameroon taxis), police car (dark blue `#1C2A5E`, one white stripe reading "POLICE" on both sides, blue/red light bar; no crest), bendskin moto-taxi (rider in a variant-colour jacket, helmet on), minibus (paper / mist with an indigo stripe, a window band all round, roof rack with luggage; generic shape, not a named model), bus, timber truck (stakes and five logs with light cut ends), banana truck (low side boards, 13 bunches in two greens; six-sided wheels to stay light), ambulance (white van, red stripe and light bar; no red cross, a protected emblem). Car colours mix palette tokens (paper, mist, silver, black, indigo, red, blue). Each type exposes its size and head / tail light points for the night glow (P3B-03). Dev only: `/?city&vehicles` (live traffic off) parks every type on the main road both ways and every sedan colour on Career Road.
- Traffic follows a lane graph generated from `layout` (deterministic, tested). Drive on the right (Cameroon). Traffic lights at the downtown crossings; vehicles keep a gap and give way at junctions; motos may filter between cars.
- Lane graph (P3B-01, `src/lib/roadGraph.js`): built from the road rectangles, so new roads join it by themselves. A vertical and a horizontal road that touch or overlap meet at a junction on their centre lines; a road end that touches no other road is an `edge`, where traffic enters and leaves (since P3B-08: the far ends of the main road and of the Limbe–Douala road, out in the outskirts). Pieces of one road laid end to end (same axis, centre line and width: a road and its road out) are joined into one line, so there is no junction between them; the joined line keeps the town piece's kind and speed. Junctions are `cross` (4 arms), `tee` (3), `corner` (2, turning) or `edge`. Each road piece between junctions has one lane each way, a quarter of the road's width right of the centre (streets 0.6, Career Road 0.75, main road 1.0). Lanes stop at a stop line: the junction box (half the crossing road's width) plus a 0.8 crosswalk on every arm of crosses and tees. Turns (left / straight / right, never straight back) are short curves through the junction. Crosses have traffic lights: north–south green 8 s, amber 2 s, all red 1 s, then east–west the same (22 s cycle), each crossing offset by a fixed amount from its position so they don't all change together. At a tee, the through road (the main road wherever it's involved) has priority and the side street gives way. No overtaking. On odd downtown sizes Career Road meets the main road at a T only 0.9 from the next T, so traffic must not enter a junction unless there's room beyond it (P3B-03). Dev only: `/?city&lanes` shows the lanes, turns, crosswalks and lights.
- Traffic (P3B-03, `src/lib/traffic.js` → `src/city/Traffic.jsx`): a pure, seeded simulation on the lane graph. Each driver follows lanes and turns (mostly straight on; turns out of the map are rarer, so traffic circulates downtown), cruises at the road's speed (main road 3.2, Career Road 2.4, streets 2.0 units/s; turns 1.3; motos a little faster, buses and trucks slower), keeps a gap to the vehicle ahead (0.4; motos 0.25) and brakes smoothly for it, for red lights and for amber when it can still stop. A driver books a turn just before the stop line and only when the junction is clear of conflicting turns (ones that merge into the same lane or pass within 0.8) and there is room beyond it, so nobody stops inside a junction; a lane too short to wait on (the 0.9 main-road stretch beside Career Road) is booked straight through. Give way: a side street waits for priority traffic near the junction, and left turns wait for oncoming traffic. After 6 s of waiting a driver claims its turn: drivers who waited less leave the junction to it, and a give-way driver pulls out in front of anyone who can still stop comfortably (claims drop when its light turns red). Vehicles never pass through each other: a driver on a turn keeps its gap to anyone who took another turn out of the same lane, and before every move each vehicle checks its full-size body (an oriented rectangle) against everyone in front of it on any road and stops if they would touch. Round a corner (a 2-arm junction) the two ways always conflict, so only one way turns at a time: a bus on the outer curve cut across to the inner one. Tested on every tier and the real city, at 30 and 10 fps: no bodies touch, nobody enters on red after the 1 s all-red gap, nobody stops inside a junction, no gridlock (the longest wait stays under 35 s). No overtaking, and motos don't filter between cars yet.
- Mix: taxi 30, bendskin 22, sedan 14, minibus 8, SUV 7, pickup 6, bus 3, timber and banana trucks 2 each, police 1.5, ambulance 1 (weights). New vehicles enter at the map edges (since P3B-08 all four are far out in the outskirts, out of the start view, equally likely) and fade in over 0.6 s; leaving ones shrink away over the last 0.6 of the road. With the longer roads about 25 of 30 vehicles are in town at any moment (5 on the roads out).
- Density follows Buea time: at most 30 vehicles (phones 15), busiest 07:00–09:00 and 16:00–19:00 WAT (all of them), about 40 % at midday, about 12 % from 01:00 to 05:00. The count eases to the target: new vehicles stop entering and the rest drive out. At night, head lights (paper) and tail-lights (ember, brighter when braking) are additive camera-facing glow sprites at the kit's light points that fade in with the street lamps (no real lights). In rain traffic slows by up to 30 % and there are up to half as many motos; pedestrians carry umbrellas (P3B-04).
- Rendering: one InstancedMesh per vehicle type (sized for the maximum) plus one each for head and tail glows: at most 13 draw calls, fewer when a type is absent or it's daytime. Instances are written every frame (no React re-renders); vehicles don't cast shadows (the shadow map is static) and ignore the pointer. Reduced motion: the vehicles stay parked where they are.
- Pedestrians (P3B-04, `src/lib/pedestrians.js` → `src/city/Pedestrians.jsx`): a pure, seeded simulation on a walk network: a loop round each city block's pavement 0.5 inside its edge (lamps stand at 0.2, buildings start at 0.7), joined by every crosswalk whose two ends land on block corners (downtown streets only; the main road isn't crossed). People walk at 0.45–0.65 units/s (30 % faster on a crosswalk), keep right and keep a 0.32 gap. At lights they start crossing only in the first 4 s of the red for the road they cross and with time to get over (turning drivers get the rest of the green); someone who has already waited a whole cycle (22 s) may start later in the red, if there is still time to get over (P3B-08: more turning traffic had people missing two windows); without lights they wait while a driver who will cross is within 4 of the stop line, for at most 8 s. Drivers never book a turn through a crosswalk someone is on (`stepTraffic`'s `crossing`, `crosswalkClear`). Nobody takes two crosswalks in a row (waiting for the second would hold up the first). Up to 40 people (phones 20): busy 07:00–19:00 WAT, about 45 % at 21:00, about 5 % from 01:00 to 05:00; rain keeps up to half indoors and everyone out carries an umbrella. People step out of / into the buildings (0.8 s fade). Two people wait in front of each taxi/bus stop while the pavements are at least 30 % busy.
- Animals (P3B-04, `src/lib/animals.js` → `src/city/Animals.jsx`): two herds of 3 goats and two flocks of 4–5 chickens on the grass either side of Career Road near the entrance (the edge of town), each in an invisible pen at the first free spot near its anchor, clear of roads, blocks, plots, lamps, signs, the About house and props. They wander to random points in the pen, then graze (goats, head down) or peck (chickens, quick bursts). At night (lamps ≥ 50 %) chickens roost (fade out) and goats stand still. They stay there (P3B-08, owner): easy to find, and an egg depends on them.
- Figures (`src/lib/figures.js` → `src/city/figureGeometry.js`): a person (about 0.47 tall: between the cars and the toy-scale props), umbrella, goat and chicken as boxes and blobs, one InstancedMesh each (4 draw calls). Clothes: plain tops (palette + a few warm colours) and dark/denim/khaki lowers, no cultural motifs (ADR-009). Legs and arms swing and heads bow in the vertex shader (`aGait` = phase, stride, head), so the CPU writes three numbers per figure a frame. No shadows. Animals take clicks (P3B-06); people don't. Dev only: `/?city&figures` lines them all up on the main road.
- Phones and weak devices (P3B-10, `crowdShare` in `quality.js`): half the vehicles, people and Market shoppers on touch devices and on any device whose first quality guess is low. Decided once on load, not when the quality monitor steps down mid-visit (a new share rebuilds the traffic and everyone would jump). Reduced motion: vehicles parked, people, animals and shoppers still, the featured billboard doesn't turn over, no follow-cam motion; drive mode still works (you move it).
- Interactions (P3B-06). Vehicles, animals and signal poles are hit-tested against simple shapes (`src/lib/cityPicking.js`: a box per vehicle at least 0.5 wide so motos can be clicked, a sphere per animal, a box per pole), not their meshes, through one invisible hit object each that joins R3F's events, so the nearest thing under the pointer wins (a building in front still takes the click). Pointer cursor on hover (mouse and pen).
  - **Follow cam:** clicking a vehicle swings the camera behind it (9 away, about 60° from straight up) and follows it; the visitor can still rotate and zoom round it. A chip at the bottom right ("Following: Taxi · Esc to leave", Stop; above the bottom-left buttons on phones) and Esc go back to the view from before. Moving with the keys stays where you are; going to a building or a place ends the follow; a vehicle that drives off the map ends it too (back to the view before). Reduced motion: the camera cuts and the vehicle is parked.
  - **Taxi rides** (`rideTo(place)` in the city store, also for the AI guide in P4-04): "Take a taxi" (bottom left) lists the places (`src/lib/places.js`: Downtown, Cinema, School, Arcade, Pitch, Market, About house when it is built); following a taxi offers "Ride in this taxi". **You ride inside the taxi, all the way (owner, 2026-10-22: full real drive, front passenger seat).** `bookTaxi`: the taxi you follow, or the free taxi in town nearest where the camera looks that can get there (never one still driving in on a road out, P3B-08), or a new one at the nearest entry. It plans the shortest way through the lane graph (a light costs 4 units), never leaves the map on the way, obeys every traffic rule, and pulls up at the drop-off (the nearest point on a lane at least 2.5 long, 1 from its ends, only on lanes town traffic can reach: never a lane in from the map edge or the road up from the T). You stay seated 1.2 s, then step out: the camera flies from your seat to the place (from the main road side, 24 away, 0.55 rad up; Downtown from the south; the Market from the entrance side; the About house from Career Road) and the taxi drives on. Rides take about 30–75 s on today's city; the chip says "Taxi to the Pitch · drag to look around · Esc to get out" with Skip (fly there now) and Get out (back to the view before).
  - **Passenger view** (`src/lib/passenger.js` → `CameraRig.jsx`, `TaxiCabin.jsx`): the camera is your eyes on the front passenger seat (right-hand side: Cameroon drives on the right, so the driver sits on the left), at the driver's head height, 70° field of view, near plane 0.01; it bobs a little with the speed. Drag (mouse or finger) or the arrows/WASD/Q/E turn your head (up to 1.6 rad either way, 0.3 up, 0.45 down); let go and it turns back to the road over 1.5 s. The camera-controls stand aside during the ride and are kept at your seat, so stepping out flies on from there. The saloon model is toy-scaled (its glass is 0.14 tall next to 0.47-tall people), so on a ride its shell and its own head/tail-light glows are hidden and `TaxiCabin` draws a roomier cabin in the flat style (1 draw call, only during a ride): a yellow hood ahead, dashboard with the driver's dials, a ring steering wheel, dark pillars, doors with yellow sills, a light roof lining, the mirror, and the driver (seat, body, arms to the wheel, head). While another vehicle is clicked during a ride it honks back but you stay in your taxi. With Sound on, an engine hum follows the speed. The driver says one of the owner's lines on pick-up and on arrival (`profile.taxiLines`, "Taxi driver lines (Pidgin)" in `docs/projects.md`; silent until written), in a 3 s bubble over the dashboard.
  - **Rides never hang:** a watchdog in the city store (independent of the 3D loop) takes you to the place if no taxi has answered after 1.5 s or the ride isn't over after 125 s (`RIDE.max` 120 s), and any error in the ride step ends the ride at the place (logged). Tested on every tier: every place reachable from every entry; rides in rush-hour traffic with crossing buttons pressed arrive in under 80 s (100 s on the 8 × 8 downtown) with no touching and no red-light running. Reduced motion: no ride, the camera cuts to the place.
  - **Honk, bleat:** a clicked vehicle says "Beep beep!" ("Pip pip!" for a moto, "Paaarp!" for buses and trucks) in a speech bubble that rides above it for 1.6 s; a goat stops, turns to look at the camera for 2.5 s and says "Mèèè!"; a hen runs 1.4 away from the click at 3.5 × its speed (staying in its pen) and says "Bwok bwok!". Bubbles: paper pill, basalt mono text; the text is also read out in the live region.
  - **Traffic lights:** a pole on every arm of every crossing, on the right of the arriving traffic just behind the crosswalk (post 1.45, basalt head with red / amber / green lamps facing the traffic, a taxi-yellow crossing button). Clicking one presses the button: a green at least 2 s old ends now (amber 2 s, then the usual 1 s all red), so the other way and the walkers get their turn sooner; at most once a cycle per crossing ("Beep! The lights change now." / "They are already changing." / "Pressed already. Patience!"). The traffic, the people and the poles share one graph per layout (`roadGraphFor`), so they all see the same lights. 2 draw calls.
  - **Sound:** off by default; a "Sound" toggle (bottom left, remembered in this browser) turns on the horns (two-tone, a moto pips, buses and trucks blare), the bleat, the clucks and the button beep, all made with Web Audio (no files). P5-08 adds the rest.
- Street props (P3B-05, `src/lib/streetProps.js` → `src/city/propGeometry.js` → `StreetProps.jsx`): placed from the layout (pure, seeded, tested on every tier). Each prop has a clearance radius (tree 0.6, palm 0.5, bench 0.6, stop 1.1, kiosk 0.6, billboard 2) and takes the first free spot from a short list of candidates, clear of roads, city blocks, district plots, lamps, the Career Road signs, the About house and every prop before it. Parks: a wooden bench on one side facing in and two leafy trees (fako-green shades) in the far corners. Palms (leaning trunk, 7 fronds) on both sides of Career Road between the lamps and along the main road's south side; benches facing Career Road in every other gap. Two taxi/bus stops (indigo roof, glass sides, bench, a taxi-yellow "TAXI / BUS" sign on a pole): on the main road east of Career Road and on Career Road's west side near the entrance, each with a "CALL BOX" kiosk beside it (paper stand, coloured band and parasol in indigo / fako-green / ember; no network brands or colours). A third call box by the fuel station. Fuel station (8 × 6, no company brand): concrete pad, paper canopy with an ember band over two pumps on the road side, a small shop behind, an ember "FUEL" sign on a pole by the road, readable from both sides. All solid props are one merged mesh (vertex colours, flat shading, cast shadows into the static shadow map); their lamps (stop light strips, kiosk bulbs, canopy and shop window) one mesh that lights up with the street lamps; their text panels one mesh on a canvas atlas that glows a little at night (like the Career signs). 3 draw calls; props ignore the pointer.
- Billboards show real content only (featured projects, GitHub numbers with their source, the weather); never invented ads. Three 3.6 × 2 boards on two posts, in a row east of Career Road 5 south of the main road, facing the start view (behind the fuel station if the row is full). Featured project (`featuredBoards`): name, stack and the live site's domain; featured [DEMO] projects are left out; with several, the board turns over every 8 s (not with reduced motion; each project has its own face in the atlas, so turning is a UV switch, not a texture upload); clicking it opens that project's panel. GitHub (`githubBoard`): commits and stars summed over non-demo projects with GitHub data, "across N GitHub repositories", "Source: GitHub API · updated <date>". Weather (`weatherBoard`): "Buea now 25 °C", the condition, "Source: Open-Meteo · <time> WAT". Board style: indigo + 35 % basalt, mist border, window-colour caption, paper headline, mist detail.
- Market (P3B-07, `src/lib/marketContent.js` + `src/lib/market.js` → `src/city/MarketPlot.jsx`, `MarketPanel.jsx`): a 13 × 9 red-earth plot west of Career Road beside the entrance (`MARKET` in `layout.js`, 4.6 from the road's edge so it clears the Career signs; the roadside keeps its lamps, palms and the west taxi/bus stop, which is the drop-off). "Market" is in the Take a taxi menu, seen from the entrance side. One stall per non-demo service in `docs/projects.md` (owner order, max 18): the first takes the front row's corner nearest Career Road; rows of up to 6 face south with an aisle in front; the east end is left for a MARKET sign turned to the entrance. Stalls are sized to the people (counter at waist height, tarp at about twice head height, front higher than back so rain runs off), with plain palette tarps (neighbours differ), goods on the counter and a shelf, a paper sign above the tarp with the service name and a tarp-colour stripe. A seller behind each counter looks about; shoppers (two per three stalls, phones half) walk their row's aisle and browse. Market hours (Buea time): opens 06:00, busiest 09:00–16:00, closed by 19:30 (no sellers or shoppers); rain keeps up to half the shoppers away; a string of three bulbs just in front of each tarp's edge (P3B-10: one bulb under the tarp didn't show from above) lights with the lamps while open. Click a stall → its panel (what's delivered, example buildings that fly to their project, "Ask for a quote" → the contact panel titled with the service and an email subject "Quote: <service>", the other stalls); hover shows the stall's name. Click a shopper → it turns round and says one of the owner's Pidgin call-outs (`marketCalls`, in turn). The on-plot "Market" button is the keyboard way in. No prices anywhere (the owner quotes per job). 4 draw calls (stalls, bulbs, sign atlas, people); hit-tested in `market.js`. No cultural motifs (ADR-009).
- Outskirts and the road out (P3B-08, `ROAD_OUT` in `layout.js`, `src/lib/outskirts.js` → `src/city/Outskirts.jsx`). Roads out (kind `out`, ash + 5 % mist, dashed centre lines) have the widths of the roads they continue, so their lanes line up: Career Road carries on 24 south past the entrance (out of the start view) to a T with a 4-wide road, Limbe to the west and Douala to the east, as Buea's road runs down to Mutengene; the main road runs on 30 past the plots each way. The road up from the T gives way there. Traffic enters and leaves only at the four far ends. `layout.bounds` stays the town (props, pens and plots keep their places); `layout.reach` lets the camera target go down to the T; `layout.outskirts` is the area the houses may use (30 beyond the plots each side, 30 north of the town towards Fako, 16 south of the T). A direction sign stands across the T facing the road out, on two posts (green board, fako-green + 25 % basalt, paper border and text, no distances): one place per row with its arrow on its own side and a paper rule between (P3B-10: on one line the names ran together from a distance): drivers coming down read "← Douala" over "Limbe →", the back reads "← Limbe" over "Douala →" from the town side; it glows a little at night.
  - Houses: small Buea-style houses (people-scaled: walls 0.85–1.2 high, 1.8–3.2 × 1.5–2.3), plaster walls in paper mixed with window / sky / fako-green / ember, or cement grey, on a cement plinth; zinc gable roofs (bare grey, rusty, or painted blue-grey) with an overhang; a door and one or two windows on the front; 30 % have a veranda (slab, two posts, a lean-to roof), 20 % a low cement yard wall with a gap for the gate. Rows face both sides of every road out (every 4.2, a few gaps), then a jittered grid (4.8) fills the rest, thinning out away from the town (75 % near it, 12 % furthest); a scattered house within 7 of a road turns to face it. They keep 2.5 from the town's bounds, off every road, apart from each other, off the sign, and out of a circle (radius 11) just south of the entrance so the start view has no house in its face. About 150 on today's city, 240 on the 8 × 8 downtown. Seeded by grid cell and position, so the same city has the same houses. 60 % have their windows lit at night (with `lighting.windows`). One merged mesh (with the sign's posts), one for the lit windows, one for the sign's faces: 3 draw calls; not clickable, no shadows.
- Shopfronts downtown (P3B-08, `src/lib/shopfronts.js` → `src/city/Shopfronts.jsx`): every project building has a shop on each of its two faces that look onto a street (blocks are 2 × 2 lots), with that project's own name on the fascia (owner choice: real content, no invented shop names). Sized to the ground floor (0.6) and the people: display window (0.34 high, about half the face, dark glass by day, warm light with the city's windows), a door, an awning (seeded per building from ember, indigo, fako-green, window + 35 % ember, sky + 35 % indigo) tilted down at 0.48–0.53, above the heads of people on the pavement, and the fascia (90 % of the face, 0.11 high, the awning colour + 45 % basalt with the name in paper; glows a little at night). Those faces have no ground-floor windows. Clicks go through to the building (opens its project); a building in X-Ray hides its shops. 3 draw calls (awnings and doors, display windows, the fascia atlas: one 512 × 64 tile per building).
- Drive mode (P3B-09, `src/lib/drive.js` → `src/city/Drive.jsx`; owner 2026-10-23): optional, off by default. "Drive" (bottom left) → Taxi or Bendskin. You start on Career Road's northbound lane by the entrance, facing downtown (further up if someone is in the way).
  - **Controls:** WASD / arrows (event.code, so AZERTY keeps the places), Space brakes, H honks, V switches the taxi between the chase view and the driver's seat, Enter opens the building you are stopped next to, Esc parks (the camera goes back to the view from before, unless it was sent somewhere else). Touch screens: a joystick replaces the bottom-left buttons (up drives on, down brakes then backs up, sideways steers). The chip (bottom right) says what you drive and has Open <project>, View (taxi), Horn and Park. Taking a taxi, following a vehicle, a building, Tab or a place ends the drive; clicking a vehicle while driving just makes it honk.
  - **Handling (arcade):** taxi top speed 4 (traffic cruises at 2–3.2), reverse 1.2; bendskin 4.4, reverse 0.8, sharper turns. Steering needs speed and tightens as you slow down. 60 % of the top speed on the grass.
  - **Where you can go:** roads and grass. Solid: city blocks with their pavements (so you never reach the people on them), district plots and the Market, the About house, the fuel station's pumps, canopy posts, shop and sign (you can drive under the canopy), taxi/bus stops, kiosks, benches, tree and palm trunks, lamps, signal poles, billboard and sign posts (boards are high enough to drive under), the animal pens, the outskirts houses (with verandas and yard walls) and the Limbe / Douala sign posts. The bounds are `layout.outskirts`; at the far ends "End of OJ City. Turn back!". The car never moves into anything: it slides along a wall at a glancing angle (with a little drag) and stops head-on; it moves at most 0.12 at a time so thin posts can't be skipped. Tested on every tier with 90 s of random driving.
  - **The traffic and you:** your car is one more instance of its type in Traffic (its lights too; no extra draw calls). Drivers who see it ahead stop behind it at their usual gap and never move into it, wherever it is (lane or junction); walkers wait while it is within 1 of their crosswalk; it can't drive into people on a crosswalk. Bump into a vehicle and it honks at you (at most every 2 s each). With Sound on, the engine hum follows your speed.
  - **Camera:** chase view 3.2 behind and 1.4 up, looking 1.6 ahead, 60° field of view; it swings round after the car turns (at once with reduced motion) and comes in closer when a building is behind you. Driver's seat (taxi): your eyes on the left, the cabin drawn without the driver and with a spoked steering wheel that turns as you steer (2 draw calls, only in that view); drag to look round; it bobs with speed (not with reduced motion). Reduced motion: drive mode works (you move it), the traffic stays parked.
  - **Eggs:** *Tour guide*: in one drive, reach every district (Downtown's streets, or within 3 of the Cinema, School, Arcade, Pitch and Market plots). *Model driver*: in one drive, go through every crossing with lights (4 on today's city, up to 16) and never enter one on red (amber is fine); entering on red says "Red light!" and clears the crossings passed so far.
  - **Police chase** (P3B-11, `src/lib/chase.js` → `Drive.jsx`, drawn by `Traffic.jsx`): running a red brings a police car after you, with its siren (two tones, 870 / 650 Hz every 0.45 s, quieter further away; Sound on only) and its roof lights flashing blue and red, five times a second, one side then the other (bright enough by day). The nearest police car in the traffic within 40 leaves it and turns after you; otherwise a new one appears on a road 20–30 away, behind you if it can. It drives with your car's handling code (`stepCar`, top speed 4.2: faster than the taxi, slower than the bendskin, and it turns wider), so it can't go through anything either; it finds its way on a 1-unit grid of the drive world (roads cost 1, grass 3; a cell is open when a 0.8 square at its centre touches nothing), replanned twice a second, and goes straight for you within 8 when the line is clear, stopping 2 from you. Stuck for 1.2 s, it backs up for 0.9 s. The traffic and the walkers give way to it as they do to you, and you can't drive through it.

**Performance (P1-08, `src/lib/quality.js`):**
- 16 draw calls for today's city (budget 100): instanced building parts, merged layout surfaces, 4 backdrop meshes, 3 for the street lamps (P2-03), +1 for rain while it rains (P2-04).
- Quality levels change only DPR and shadow-map size, never which shaders exist (turning shadows off recompiles every material, a long freeze on a phone): low = DPR 1 + 512 map, medium = 1.25 + 1024, high = 1.5 + 2048. DPR never exceeds 1.5.
- Starting level from the device: ≤ 2 cores or ≤ 2 GB → low; ≤ 4 cores or ≤ 4 GB → medium (low on touch); otherwise high (medium on touch). Unknown values (no `deviceMemory` in Safari/Firefox) don't count against the device.
- `npm run perf` (P3B-10) pins Buea time to 17:30 by default (`?perf&time=HH:MM`, production build, perf runs only): rush hour at dusk, every vehicle and person out with the lamps and head/tail-light glows on, the heaviest the city gets, so a run means the same whenever it's made (`--time=HH:MM` or `--time=live` to change it). Three samples per profile, each within budget: the start view, the flight to the first building and zoomed right out. `--reduced-motion` also fails if any vehicle or person moves; `--weather=storm` adds the rain.
- After the first frame, drei `PerformanceMonitor` steps down one level after ~5 s mostly under 40 fps. It never steps back up: each change resizes the canvas and redraws shadows, so flip-flopping costs more than it saves.
- Shadows are static: the light and buildings don't move, so the shadow map is drawn only when the layout or quality changes. The moving sun (P2-02) redraws it for one frame once it has moved more than 0.3°, about once a minute.
- No `backdrop-filter` blur or blurred `box-shadow` on UI over the canvas. A blur is recomputed every frame the canvas changes, and the label's first shadow cost a ~150 ms frame. Use opaque-ish backgrounds and 1 px borders/rings.

## Time of day (Africa/Douala, UTC+1)
The sun's real position in Buea (NOAA equations in `shared/solar.js`; it sits on the horizon at the same sunrise/sunset as `/api/buea`, within ~1 min) on Buea's clock: the device clock corrected by `/api/buea`'s `now`, or the device clock alone if the API fails. Keyframes:
| Phase | Sky | Sun/ambient | City |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night | basalt → deep indigo | moonlight, low | all lamps + most windows on |
| Dawn | indigo → pink → mist | warm, rising | lamps fading |
| Day | bright clear blue (`--sky`), pale horizon | bright neutral | windows off, beacons visible |
| Dusk | orange → indigo | warm low sun | lamps switching on one by one |

**Implementation (P2-02, `src/lib/dayNight.js`):**
- Phases by sun elevation: night < −10°, day ≥ 8°, dawn/dusk in between (dawn while the sun is rising). In Buea (mid-October) dawn runs ~05:30–06:45 and dusk ~17:35–18:45.
- Keyframes by elevation, blended linearly. Morning: −12° Night → −6° pre-dawn (indigo, ember glow at the horizon) → 0° Dawn (pink horizon) → 8° morning (mist, sky + 40 % mist) → 20° Day (zenith `--sky`, horizon sky + 60 % paper). Evening: 20° Day → 8° golden (sky + 25 % indigo over a window-warm horizon) → 0° Dusk (orange: ember + 35 % window) → −6° blue hour (indigo + ember) → −12° Night. Every colour is a mix of the palette tokens; values in the code.
- Light (one directional, always): the sun follows its real azimuth (east → south → west); its height is limited to 6–60° for shadows, as Buea's noon sun is ~75–90° up and flattens the city. Intensity 0 at −6°, 0.9–1 at 0°, 1.6 at 8°, 2.8 by day. Below −6° it becomes a fixed moonlight (south-east, 45° up, cool mist-indigo, 0.8), not the real moon. Both are dark at the swap, so it doesn't show.
- Ambient (one ambient light, always): 0.6 at night, 0.8 at dawn/dusk, 0.9 in the morning and golden hour, 1.15 by day; colour mist, tinted ember/window around sunrise and sunset, indigo at night.
- Updates every minute; the scene eases to the new values (time constant 0.6 s), instantly with reduced motion. The first frame is a cut (never a fade from night). Lights are never added or removed (that would recompile every material).
- Weather (P2-04) adjusts these values before they are eased; see Weather → Implementation.

**City lights (P2-03, `cityLightsAt` in `src/lib/dayNight.js`, `src/lib/lamps.js`, `src/city/Lamps.jsx`):**
- Two levels (0..1) by sun elevation, linear: windows 0 at +6° → 1 at −4°; lamps 0 at +2° → 1 at −6°. The same ramps run backwards at dawn, so windows come on first at dusk (~17:45 in mid-October) and lamps follow (~18:00–18:35); at dawn lamps go off first (gone by ~06:20) and the last windows by ~06:35. Day = all off, night = all on. Eased with the rest of the lighting (instant with reduced motion).
- One by one: every lit window and every lamp has a seeded switch-on point in [0, 0.9] (window: slug; lamp: its position) and fades in over 0.04 of the level as the level passes it. A per-instance attribute + `onBeforeCompile` patch (`src/city/switchable.js`), so no extra draw calls.
- Night windows: which and how many are lit is unchanged (push recency, 0.1–0.9; a real number, not "most windows on"). By day all windows are dark glass, `#1B1F2A` + 30 % of the horizon colour; at night unlit windows are plain `#1B1F2A`.
- Lamps: on every block's pavement, 0.2 inside the edge, at the 4 corners and 4 edge midpoints (every 4 units); both sides of Career Road every 5; the main road's south side every 6 (not at Career Road's mouth) and its north side beside the district plots. 99 lamps today. A 1.3-high ash post, a `--window` head (grey when off) and a 3.2-wide round pool of light on the ground (additive, 0.4 peak, unfogged). No real lights: ~100 point lights would be far too slow on phones.
- No bloom: a full-screen pass breaks the phone budget. May come back later as a high-quality-only extra.
- Under a dark sky they switch on earlier: as if the sun were up to 5° lower at full gloom (see Weather), so a stormy late afternoon lights up while a bright one doesn't.

## Weather (Open-Meteo WMO codes)
| Codes | Meaning | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | clear | clean sky |
| 2–3 | cloudy | cloud layer, softer light |
| 45, 48 | fog | **Fako mist**: heavy fog band on the mountain, lighter in the city |
| 51–67, 80–82 | drizzle / rain / showers | rain particles, wet road reflections |

Buea is often misty: keep a light mist band on Fako even in clear weather.

**Implementation (P2-04, `src/lib/weatherFx.js`, `src/city/Rain.jsx`):**
- Levels: `weatherFx(/api/buea weather)` → cloud, rain, fog (0..1), storm, and gloom (0..1, how much darker the city gets: 0.55 cloud + 0.35 rain + 0.1 fog + 0.15 storm). Cloud is the measured cover when there is one, else by code (0 → 0, 1 → 0.15, 2 → 0.45, 3 → 0.85); rain by code (light drizzle 0.2 … heavy rain/showers 1), raised by the measured mm (6 mm/h = 1). Missing, unknown or fallback weather draws the plain clear city. Snow (never in Buea) is a grey, misty day.
- Light (`applyWeather`, inside `lightingAt`, eased with day/night): the sky goes towards an overcast grey (the time of day's own sky, desaturated, darkened with gloom): zenith × 0.85 cloud, horizon and fog × 0.7 of cloud or fog. Direct sun −80 % and ambient −20 % at full gloom, both desaturated with cloud: overcast light is soft, with faint shadows. Clear weather changes nothing.
- Clouds: drawn in the sky shader (no extra draw call), drifting noise on a flat ceiling, faded towards the horizon; coverage follows cloud.
- Fog: scene fog closes in from 60–600 to 20–260 at full fog, and to 65 % of that in heavy rain. The Fako mist band's density = 1 + 1.6 fog + 0.6 cloud, so fog hides most of the mountain while the city stays readable.
- Rain: one line mesh (1 draw call while raining, 0 otherwise), drops animated in the vertex shader in an 80 × 40 × 80 box in front of the camera that wraps as it moves. Drop count = rain × 600 / 1000 / 1500 (low / medium / high quality). Drops are pale against the day sky and faint grey at night. Frozen with reduced motion.
- Wet streets: in rain, roads and pavements darken up to 35 % and lose roughness (0.95 → 0.4), so the sun leaves a soft sheen. No real reflections (too slow on phones).
- Lightning (thunderstorm only): one strike per 14 s slot, 3–9 s into it, so strikes are 8–20 s apart; a strike is one or two 0.12 s flashes 0.2 s apart, so never more than 3 flashes a second (WCAG 2.3.1). It brightens the ambient light and the sky. Seeded by time, so the same for everyone. None with reduced motion.
- Cost: storm = 1 extra draw call. `npm run perf -- --weather=storm` checks it (19 desktop / 17 phone draw calls, 60 fps).

## Motion
- GSAP for UI, R3F frame loop for the scene. Ease: `power2.out` for UI, critically-damped springs for the camera.
- Durations: UI 200–400 ms; camera flights 1.2–1.8 s.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no camera flights (cut instead), no lightning, no auto-rotation.

## Culture (Bayangi / Manyu): read before using motifs
Visual inspiration can come from Manyu textiles and art (for example indigo resist-dye patterns) for the loader, dividers and textures.
**Some Manyu symbols (certain Nsibidi signs, Ekpe/Mgbe regalia such as ukara cloth) carry sacred or restricted meaning.**
Use only motifs the owner has confirmed with community members are appropriate for public use (ADR-009). When in doubt, use abstract geometry.
